Application process

A clear three-step process

Use the official position reference number throughout your application and retain copies of every document submitted.

1

Find a position

Search the vacancy register by division, position title, reference number, advertised location, or classification.

2

Read the job description

Confirm the duties, requirements, advertised location, classification, base salary, and reference number before preparing your application.

3

Submit one complete application

Complete every required field and attach one compiled application PDF containing your CV and supporting certificates.

Before you apply

Prepare your documents

  • Compile your current CV and qualification certificates into one readable PDF
  • Add relevant training or award certificates to the same PDF, where available
  • Keep the final compiled PDF at 10 MB or less
  • A recent passport-style photograph in JPG or PNG format
  • Accurate contact information and referee details

Important applicant notice

Only applications submitted during an advertised application period should be considered. Shortlisted applicants may be contacted using the phone number or email supplied.
