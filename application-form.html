Application Process

How to apply

All interested applicants are required to submit their applications strictly through the online portal at dodcareers.link.

Required Documents

Prepare before submission

Form RS 3.2 is completed electronically in this online application. You do not need to complete and upload a separate RS 3.2 form unless the Department specifically requests it.

  • Expression of Interest (EOI)
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Certificates of Educational Qualifications
  • References
  • Other relevant supporting documents, including one (1) valid form of identification such as a Passport, PNG Driver's Licence, NID, Work ID or Student ID.

Form RS 3.2 Cross-reference

Digital application mapping

This online application is the electronic recruitment version of Form RS 3.2. The information from the paper form is captured across the relevant online steps rather than repeated on one page.

  • Position and personal particulars: Steps 1 and 3.
  • Employment record, Armed Forces service, convictions and previous applications: Step 4.
  • Secondary education, qualifications, further study, publications and professional information: Step 5.
  • Civic activities, interests, sports and recreation: Step 6.
  • Referees and supporting documents: Step 7. For this recruitment exercise, provide the three referees requested by the online portal.
  • Applicant declaration: Step 8.

Advertisement source: Department of Defence Position Vacancies portal — dodcareers.link.

Submission Format

Subject and PDF requirements

  1. EOI subject line: State the Position Reference Number and Designation/Position Title. Example: DEX.004_Ministry Liasion Officer
  2. Compiled application: Combine all application documents into one (1) PDF file not exceeding 10 MB.
  3. PDF filename: Use the format Reference Number_Position Title_Your Surname. Example: DEX.004_Ministry Liasion Officer_Taureka
