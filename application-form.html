Application instructions

Read the official application process and instructions carefully before proceeding.

Application Process

All interested applicants are required to submit their applications strictly through the online portal at https://dodcareers.link.

Instructions for Applicants

  1. All applicants are required to submit the following documents:
    • Expression of Interest (EOI) addressed to:
      Addressed to
      Secretary
      Department of Defence
      Free Mail Bag, Boroko,
      Port Moresby, N.C.D
      Attention
      First Assistant Secretary
      Human Resource Management
    • Completed Form RS 3.2
    • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
    • Certificates of Educational Qualifications
    • References
    • Other relevant supporting documents, including a copy of one (1) valid form of identification (for example: Passport, PNG Driver's Licence, NID, Work or Student ID).
  2. The subject line of your EOI must state the Position Reference Number and Designation/Position Title. Example: Subject: DEX.008_Deputy Secretary - Policy Wing
  3. All application documents must be compiled into one (1) PDF file not exceeding 10 MB.
  4. The PDF file must be named in the following format: Reference Number_Position Title_Your Surname. Example: DEX.008_Deputy Secretary - Policy Wing_Taureka
  5. For queries and clarification, please contact the Department of Defence Recruitment Team:
    Email: dodrecruitment@defence.gov.pg
    Phone: 324 2198
Approved by Hari John Akipe Secretary of Defence
