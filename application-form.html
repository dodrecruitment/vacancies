Application instructions

Read each instruction carefully before proceeding.

  1. Submit one complete application for each position you wish to apply for.
  2. Use the exact position title and reference number from the official vacancy register.
  3. Upload one clear identification document: National ID, passport, driver's licence, or birth certificate.
  4. Compile your Expression of Interest or cover letter, CV, qualification certificates, and any relevant training or award certificates into one clear, readable PDF.
  5. The identification document must be 5 MB or less. The compiled application PDF must be 10 MB or less. Password-protected PDFs cannot be processed.
  6. Ensure your phone number and email address are accurate and remain active.
  7. Providing false or misleading information may result in disqualification.
  8. Only shortlisted applicants may be contacted for screening, interview, or assessment.
