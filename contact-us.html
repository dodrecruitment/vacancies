Application and EOI Guidance

The information below matches the instructions provided in the online application form.

Online application process

All interested applicants are required to submit their applications strictly through the official online recruitment portal.

Form RS 3.2 is completed electronically in the online application. A separate RS 3.2 form does not need to be completed and uploaded unless the Department specifically requests it.

Required documents

Your compiled application should contain:

  • Expression of Interest (EOI)
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Certificates of Educational Qualifications
  • References
  • Other relevant supporting documents, including one valid form of identification such as a Passport, PNG Driver's Licence, NID, Work ID or Student ID.

Submission format

  1. EOI subject line: state the Position Reference Number and Designation/Position Title.
  2. Compiled application: combine all application documents into one (1) PDF file not exceeding 10 MB.
  3. PDF filename: use Reference Number_Position Title_Your Surname.
When reporting an online portal issue: include your name, the position reference number, the page where the issue occurred, and a screenshot if available. Do not send passwords or other account credentials.
